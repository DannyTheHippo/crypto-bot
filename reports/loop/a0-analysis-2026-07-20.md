## Ranked findings

### 1. Consult scheduler delivers ~1-3 wakes/day vs the 10-20/day design intent — the single binding constraint on the promotion gate

**So what:** Every downstream X-step (feeds, reflection, post-mortems, attribution) enriches
consults that almost never happen — fix the scheduler floor first or the 14-day window stretches
to 2-4+ months and the whole learning stack stays starved.

**Evidence:** spot-consults.csv lines 638-643: exactly ONE post-epoch spot wake in ~11.8h
(2026-07-20 01:00Z, 6 symbols, all hold); perp-consults.csv lines 154-157: 4 consults in 33.5h
with a 21.5h awake gap (journal running per perp-decisions-hourly.csv lines 100-121); spot-
consult-gate.txt skipped_scheduled=336 vs ~6 consults executed; the 16-bar (4h) fallback
demonstrably did not fire for 6.75-21.5h awake stretches, and wake-on-move 1.5% cannot trigger
inside the 0.47% BTC chop band that dominated the sample (perp-consults.csv lines 138-151).
Promotion arithmetic (promotion-readiness.service.ts:15-16,90-102): >=30 trips over >=14 trade-
anchored days = >=2.14 closed trips/day; at historical entry fractions (3.8% perp / ~9% spot) that
needs 24-56 consults/day — current pace yields an expected ~0 entries/day and observed 0. Cost is
NOT the limiter: one full 6-symbol wake costs ~$0.06-0.18, and sparse cadence actually pays a 2-4x
cache-write premium (perp-consults.csv lines 154-157: cwrite ~5,950 / cread 0 on every post-epoch
consult) — denser cadence is partly self-funding.

**So what:** Tuning cadence knobs against a broken counter or an unscoped fallback repeats the

**So what:** Adding feeds under current aggregation semantics mechanically LOWERS entry rate

### 4. Promotion gate math is a cumulative ratchet: the 14-day window is trade-anchored at BOTH ends, every idle consult permanently raises the net-PnL bar, and an epoch re-stamp is costless RIGHT NOW but expensive the moment trading resumes

**So what:** Ship the activation fixes, then re-stamp both epochs at verified flat instants —
otherwise the -$2.90-and-counting idle burn is permanently baked into the promotion denominator
and slow evidence is doubly penalized.

**Evidence:** promotion-readiness.service.ts:90-102: windowDays anchors on first/last closed-cycle
timestamps — idle time never advances it; :65-82: llmTokenTotals(epochMs) has no trailing window,

epoch. Both lanes are currently flat with 0 trips since epoch (spot-equity.csv rows 2-236, perp-
equity.csv rows 2-501), satisfying the straddle-bound flat-instant precondition (service lines
43-60; state.md:126-128) at zero evidence cost. The bar at the 14-day floor: ~$0.7-1.0 realized
per trip over 30 trips on the $1k book, rising with campaign length.

## Risks

Manufactured negative-edge trades: XA1+XA3 together raise cadence AND loosen the entry mandate —
v1 at full cadence lost money on its trades (inverted R:R, ~-$0.93/trip era), and the gate needs
positive net PnL, not just 30 trips. Overshooting converts an evidence problem into a realized-
loss problem. Mitigation is built in: per-trip net-of-cost WATCH (X9), the XA1 stop-tuning rule,
and sizing that modulates rather than gates — but if the first ~10 v2 trips run materially
negative net of fees, pause the loosening, not the cadence.

Cost re-blowout under the fixed breakers: 8+ consults/day at degraded cache hit rates, a still-
unscoped fallback, or an unfixed reflection loop could re-starve the lanes inside the unchanged
$1.50/$0.75 stops. XA1's menu-scoping and XA2's reservation must land and verify BEFORE the
cadence knobs turn, or the first fallback wave repeats the $2.16 blackout.

Epoch re-stamp integrity: XA7 is costless exactly once, at flat, now. If re-stamping becomes a
habit after every config change it functions as a ratchet-escape that voids the evidence program's
credibility; and stamping spot before the perp campaign config settles (or vice versa) re-accrues
idle burn under a superseded config. One stamp per lane, decision-recorded, at that lane's final
pre-campaign config.

Anti-ratchet over-correction: capping how much reflection may tighten filters and scoring skipped
winners as errors could block legitimate learning from real losses and swing the playbook toward
chasing — the exact behavior the maker-entry study proved costly (~354bps of dodged losses). The
X7 population split (grade hold decisions, never unfilled maker orders) is the guardrail; verify
the first reflection pass respects it.

Perp short activation adds risks the long book never had: funding drag on shorts (now fail-CLOSED
in the gate), squeeze risk in chop, and two-sided exposure on a 2x book. If the first shorts
cluster in the dead 0.47%-band regime, the mandate change produces fee-bleed round trips that
satisfy the trip counter while dragging netPnl negative.

XA6/X2 channel tiering could starve the scanner or trigger staleness vetoes: REST-served rankings
may lag ws-served ones, and menu churn now implies subscribe churn at promotion time — if a symbol
enters the menu and its full channels take seconds to attach, RISK_STALE_MAX_AGE_MS=5000 vetoes
exactly the consults the scheduler just forced. The XA6 acceptance (no null scores, reconnect rate
at baseline) is load-bearing, not decorative.

Portfolio-consult flip on perp changes the call shape wholesale (batching, 3000ms coalescing
window, menu-6 payloads) — the W6 forced-outcome-without-consult anomaly is exactly the defect
class a batching seam breeds. If XA1's accounting reconciliation is skipped or inconclusive, X2
ships a wider lane on an unverified scheduler.

nextConsultBars cap 64→32 overrides model self-scheduling: if the model was choosing long delays
correctly in dead regimes, forced wakes buy holds at consult cost (the hold tax). XA4's persisted
directives are the check — if post-fix data shows the model already requesting short delays, the
binding knob was the fallback floor and the cap should be reverted rather than defended.

Confounded attribution: the XA bundle, playbook revision, epoch re-stamps, and X2 widening all
land within days of each other, so X8's per-version table cannot cleanly separate cadence effects
from mandate effects from menu effects for the first campaign. Accepted deliberately (trips are
