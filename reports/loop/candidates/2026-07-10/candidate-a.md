## regime notes

Tradeable regimes are rare; holding is the default. A long is only worth considering when the
base-interval trend and momentum agree (EMA fast above EMA slow, RSI14 between 52 and 68) AND the
higher-timeframe trend does not contradict it. Use the derivatives block when present: funding
near zero or positive with a stable or widening premium basis is constructive; deeply negative
funding or a widening discount basis means positioned sellers — hold, whatever the chart says.
Choppy tape (EMAs repeatedly crossing, RSI hugging 50) is untradeable noise. The faster movers
(SOL, XRP, LINK) swing harder than BTC/ETH: demand cleaner confirmation and expect wider stops,
which raises the bar their expected move must clear.

## entry rules

Enter long only when ALL hold: (1) the expected favorable move is at least three times the stated
round-trip cost — at 20bps cost, do not act for less than roughly a 60bps expected move; (2) a
fresh closed bar confirms the move (never a single wick); (3) RSI14 is 52-68 — above 68 the move
is already paid for; (4) the derivatives block, when present, is not hostile (no deeply negative
funding, no widening discount basis); (5) the order-book spread is small relative to the expected
move. One attempt per setup: if the entry thesis fails, do not re-enter the same level within a
few bars.

## exit rules

Place the protective stop under the structure that justified the entry, and never widen it. Set
the take-profit distance to at least 1.8 times the stop distance — history here shows winners
smaller than losers, which loses even with a good hit rate; the plan must invert that. Exit early,
before either level, when the thesis breaks: EMA fast closes back below EMA slow, RSI14 drops
below 45, or funding flips sharply negative while price stalls. If several bars pass with neither
level approached and momentum flattening, exit on the next favorable print rather than paying the
time cost of a dead position.

## mistakes to avoid

Every round trip costs about 20bps plus spread; a thin edge is a losing edge — skip it. Do not
average into a losing position. Do not chase a breakout that already ran past typical overbought
levels. Do not give back most of an open gain waiting for confirmation that the move resumed:
tighten the exit once a position has moved a full cost-multiple in profit. Do not read one strong
candle as a regime change. When the derivatives block and the chart disagree, trust the block and
hold.
