## 5. Validation-standard evidence (Step D) — the edge verdict

**Pre-registered grid** (declared before seeing results, exitZ fixed at 0 = canonical revert-to-mean, NOT tuned):
lookback {20, 50, 100} × entryZ {1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5} = 12 combos, across 1m/5m/15m/1h = **48 variants this study**.
Real Binance BTC/USDT OHLCV; fee 10 bps/side (conservative) + zero-fee cross-check; next-bar-open fills (no lookahead);
chronological IS (first 70%) / OOS (last 30%); PnL via the real `applyFillToPosition`. Full table in
`reports/nightly/meanrev-study.md`.

**Result — documented REJECTION:**
- **0 / 48** variants positive in BOTH IS and OOS at 10 bps/side.
- **6 / 48** positive in both even at **ZERO fees** (all on the 5m interval) — so a near-zero count is the STRATEGY, not
  transaction costs (the decisive zero-fee cross-check).
- Every interval's IS-best **FAILS OOS** (1h 100/1.0: IS +4.58% → OOS −4.99%; 15m 50/2.5: −8.13% → −0.97%;
  5m 100/2.5: −2.33% → −1.03%; 1m 100/2.5: −4.08% → −0.77%).
- Signature: high win-rates (60–73% on 1h/15m) with **negative** returns — the classic mean-reversion failure mode
  (many small wins erased by fat-tail losses when dips keep dipping / trend continues against the position).

**Deflated-Sharpe / MinBTL framing:** with cumulative N = 208 trials across the research program, a deployable edge would
need to clear a t-stat hurdle ~3.0 and the deflated Sharpe / MinBTL bar. **No variant is even positive out-of-sample**, so
the hypothesis does not approach the standard — there is nothing to deflate. Per step D, a poor OOS result CLOSES the
hypothesis; re-tuning the same plain z-score form on this data would convert the holdout into in-sample data (forbidden).

**Verdict: the plain short-horizon z-score mean-reversion hypothesis is CLOSED** — no robust, fee-surviving edge on
BTC/USDT across the tested intervals/params, the same verdict class as EMA-cross. This is real research value (a rigorously
evidenced negative), not a failed night.
